05 / QUICK ANSWERS

Good to
know.

Some details depend on your device and the app version you're using. These are useful starting points.

Does Halloween AI work offline?

Some on-device features can work offline when the required model and files are already available. Online services such as web search, model downloads and connected-account features need an internet connection.

Does every feature use the cloud?

No. The app may support on-device processing as well as optional online services. Check the Privacy Policy for details about specific features and their data flows.

Can I use my own models?

The app includes model discovery and import capabilities for supported formats. Compatibility, performance and licensing depend on the model and your device.

Where can I read the legal information?

Use the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions links in the navigation or footer. Both pages are part of this website.

How do I contact the team?

Email support.halloweenai@gmail.com for questions, feedback or support requests.
